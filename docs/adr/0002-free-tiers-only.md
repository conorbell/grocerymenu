# Stay on free tiers: no paid services

The app must cost nothing to run. It lives on Vercel Hobby and the Supabase free plan, and new features must fit inside them rather than add a paid plan or a new paid service. In practice this means photos are resized in the browser before upload (~800px JPEG) and deleted from storage when replaced or when their Meal is deleted, and we accept the free plan's pause after about a week without use.
